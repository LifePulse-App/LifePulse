Community Guidelines

Keep StreakSphere Safe & Authentic

At StreakSphere, we are building a new era of social media centered around authentic connections, real-time moods, and verified accountability. To keep this community thriving and safe, we require all users to adhere to the following guidelines.

1. Be Respectful & Kind

We do not tolerate harassment, bullying, or abuse. You may not use StreakSphere to:

  • Attack, threaten, or intimidate other users.
  • Post hate speech or discriminate against individuals based on race, ethnicity, national origin, religion, sexual orientation, caste, sex, gender identity, or serious disease/disability.
  • Encourage others to engage in targeted harassment or dogpiling.

2. Stay Authentic

StreakSphere is about real effort and genuine connections. Cheating the system ruins the experience for everyone. You must not:

  • Use bots, automated scripts, or emulators to farm AI-verified activity points or artificially inflate relationship streaks.
  • Create fake accounts to manipulate leaderboards or impersonate other people, brands, or organizations.
  • Upload deceptive proof of activity to bypass our AI verification engine.

3. Protect Privacy

Respect the physical and digital boundaries of others. Do not:

  • Post private, confidential, or personally identifying information (doxxing) without explicit consent.
  • Share intimate images or videos of someone without their knowledge and consent.
  • Misuse the AR Spatial Chat or Live MoodMap to stalk or harass users in real life.

4. Keep it Legal and Safe

If it is illegal in real life, it is illegal on StreakSphere. We strictly prohibit:

  • Child Exploitation: We have a zero-tolerance policy for Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) or the grooming of minors. Please see our Child Safety Standards.
  • Self-Harm: Content that encourages, glorifies, or promotes suicide, self-harm, or eating disorders will be removed.
  • Illegal Goods: Buying, selling, or facilitating the trade of illegal drugs, weapons, or illicit services.
  • Violence & Gore: Graphic violence, terrorist content, or threats of mass harm.

5. Reporting and Enforcement

We empower our community to help us enforce these rules. Our moderation team uses both AI-assisted tools and human review to ensure safety.

  • How to Report: Use the in-app "Report" tools available on profiles, feed posts, and chats. Every report is reviewed to determine if a violation occurred.
  • Admin Intervention: Posts that violate community guidelines may be flagged and removed ("Admin Removed") by our moderation team to ensure a safe environment.
  • Appeals: If you believe your content or account was flagged in error, you may submit an appeal via the Help Center.
  • Account Deletion: You have full control over your data. You can request a permanent account deletion, which includes a secure OTP verification process to prevent unauthorized removal.

6. Relationship Moderation

StreakSphere offers tools to manage your relationship streaks with care:

  • Suspension (Grace Period): If you and your partner need space, you can "Suspend" your relationship. This starts a 36-hour grace period during which your streak is preserved.
  • Patch-up: During the suspension period, you can choose to "Patch-up" to restore your relationship status without loss of progress.
  • Instant Break-up: Users with StreakSphere+ have the option to bypass the grace period and end a relationship instantly.
