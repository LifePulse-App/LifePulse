Installation Guide

Get Running in Minutes

Android is a single tap. iOS requires sideloading — follow the guide and you'll be inside StreakSphere in under five minutes.

Android

📱 Android

  1. Tap "Download APK" and save the file.
  2. Open your Downloads app and tap the file.
  3. Allow "Install Unknown Apps" if prompted in Settings.
  4. Tap Install — it takes just a few seconds.
  5. Launch StreakSphere and begin your first streak!
✓ Recommended

🍎 iOS via AltStore

  1. Install AltStore on your Mac or Windows PC from altstore.io.
  2. Connect your iPhone via USB cable.
  3. Open AltStore on your computer and sign in with your Apple ID.
  4. Open AltStore on your iPhone and tap "My Apps".
  5. Tap "+" and choose the downloaded StreakSphere IPA.
  6. Wait for installation — then launch and enjoy!
Alternative

🍎 iOS via Sideloadly

  1. Download Sideloadly from sideloadly.io on your computer.
  2. Connect your iPhone via USB cable.
  3. Drag the StreakSphere IPA file into Sideloadly.
  4. Enter your Apple ID — used only for code signing.
  5. Click Start and wait for the process to complete.
  6. Go to Settings → VPN & Device Management and trust the profile.

ℹ️ Important iOS Notice

Apple requires all third-party apps to be signed before installation. With a free Apple ID, apps need re-signing every 7 days — this is an Apple limitation, not a StreakSphere issue. AltStore can auto-renew in the background when on the same Wi-Fi as your computer.
