Child Safety Standards

Last Updated: July 2026

StreakSphere is committed to maintaining a safe environment for all users. We have a strict, zero-tolerance policy against Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) and the exploitation of minors.

1. Zero Tolerance Policy

Any content that depicts, encourages, promotes, or relates to the sexual exploitation or abuse of children is strictly prohibited on StreakSphere. This includes, but is not limited to, sharing CSAM, grooming behaviors, and the sexualization of minors.

2. Enforcement and Consequences

If we discover any user violating these standards, we will take immediate action, which includes:

  • Permanent termination of the user's StreakSphere account.
  • Preservation of associated data for law enforcement.
  • Reporting the incident and user details to relevant national and international authorities, including the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and local law enforcement.

3. Reporting Mechanisms

We empower our community to help keep StreakSphere safe. Users can report inappropriate content or suspicious behavior directly within the app using the "Report" button on profiles, posts, and messages.

4. Contact Information

If you have questions regarding our child safety practices, or wish to report a concern directly to our safety team, please contact our designated Child Safety Point of Contact at: aligee512@gmail.com.
